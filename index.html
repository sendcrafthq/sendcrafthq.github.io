Sendcraft YouTube Uploader

A private-use tool for uploading owner-approved videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

The tool uses Google's authorization flow and the YouTube Data API. Uploads are private. Public publication or scheduling is handled separately in YouTube Studio.

Access

This is not a public signup service. Access is limited to the account owner and channels they authorize. Connecting a channel does not authorize an upload.

Read the privacy policy and terms of service before granting access.
