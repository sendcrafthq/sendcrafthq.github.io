Sendcraft YouTube Uploader

Privacy policy

Sendcraft YouTube Uploader is an internal, owner-operated tool. It is not a service for collecting data from the public.

Data used

The tool processes OAuth credentials, authorized YouTube channel IDs and names, selected video files and thumbnails, upload metadata, and upload status records. It reads channel information to check that each upload targets the intended channel.

Purpose and storage

Data is used only to connect the owner's channels, upload approved content privately, and verify the upload. OAuth credentials are stored in encrypted GitHub repository secrets. The owner's selected files and upload records are kept in a restricted Google Drive queue. Execution runs on GitHub Actions.

Sharing

Data is sent to Google and GitHub as needed to provide authorization, storage, execution and upload services. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties. Access is limited to authorized account and repository access.

Retention and control

Credentials remain until the owner removes or replaces them, or revokes access. Files and upload records remain until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the app's Google access in their Google Account and remove stored credentials from GitHub.

Public website

These pages do not use an app signup form or app analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process request information under GitHub's own privacy policy.
